## What the target regions were and how they were chosen

The target list came only from `processed-data/11_eQTL_coloc/seurat/final/coloc_results.xlsx`, sheet `coloc_pass`. It contained 100 unique disorder/context/gene/lead-SNP comparisons. These were the final single-causal ABF rows categorized as `strong_coloc` because PP4 was greater than 0.8 and then retained only when the prior-sensitivity calculation had no error, contained plausible grid points, and passed `H4 > 0.8` for at least 50 percent of p12 grid points between 5e-6 and 5e-5. Thus, the targets were sensitivity-gated ABF findings, not all tested genes, a significance-filtered SNP list, or a manually expanded candidate-gene set.

The 100 rows represented 54 unique genes, 91 gene-context eQTL fits, 58 disorder-gene pairs, and 47 overlapping genomic regions. For each gene-context fit, the region was defined before SuSiE fitting as the minimum through maximum position of every dense tensorQTL nominal cis variant for that gene. Overlapping gene intervals were collapsed only to reuse extraction and reference work; every disorder/context/gene comparison remained distinct.

Five approved target rows were MAPK3: BD Astro, BD Inhb, BD L2.3, BD L5, and MDD Inhb. The pilot also evaluated the MAPK3 locus across all eight all-donor contexts and all three disorders as diagnostics, including the scientifically relevant SCZD distinct-signal comparison, but these diagnostic comparisons did not enlarge the final 100-row target set. Once the pilot methods reconciled, the remaining 95 approved rows were run.

The handoff, project README, covariate preparation, tensorQTL source, current official coloc and susieR documentation, and current 1000 Genomes documentation were read and reconciled. This established that `runsusie()` wraps `susie_rss()` rather than providing an independent fitter, dense `map_nominal` beta/SE values are appropriate summary statistics, and the direct-versus-wrapper comparison is an input and argument translation check.
