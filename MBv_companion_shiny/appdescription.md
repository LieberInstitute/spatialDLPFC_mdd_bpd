> This is a companion app for [Thompson et al. 2026](http://www.google.com), designed to let users easily view how their favorite gene was expressed across our diagnosis-by-sex groups and domain annotations. 

In the **"Pseudobulk Expression Plots"** tab users can view violin plots of gene expression data that provide the results of the gene-level omnibus *F*-test and are annotated with statistical significance of *t*-test results for specific diagnosis-by-sex group contrasts. 

To provide users with necessary context, we have provided here a project overview and have additional pipeline information in the **"Pseudobulk Methods Details"** tab. 

For a full description of project methods, please see our [publication in Cell](http://www.google.com) or visit the project [Github](https://github.com/LieberInstitute/spatialDLPFC_mdd_bpd/tree/devel). 

For a complete list of statistics for all tested genes please see our [Supplementary Tables](http://www.google.com).
