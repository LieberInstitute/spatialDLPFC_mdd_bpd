Prior to DE modeling, we removed genes that were lowly expressed in SRT but had been retained due to high expression in snRNAseq. This was accomplished with <a href="https://rdrr.io/bioc/edgeR/man/filterByExpr.html" target="_blank">edgeR</a> filters, taking the intersection of genes that passed minimum expression criteria based on donor-level and domain-level assessment. Library size normalization factors were re-generated based only on genes that passed the filtering criteria. Raw counts and <a href="https://rdrr.io/bioc/edgeR/man/calcNormFactors.html" target="_blank">edgeR normalization factors</a> were passed as input for DE models.

Despite violin plots of gene expression being available for 21,080 genes, only the subset of genes included in DE modeling have statistics available. In lieu of *F*-test statistics, genes that were not included in DE models will appear with the disclaimer “gene not run in model, excluded by edgeR filters”. Again, owing to the differences in domains, a small number of genes were included in the domain~SP~ DE models and not the domain~CT~ DE models, and vice versa.
