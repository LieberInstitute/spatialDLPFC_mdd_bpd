Following spot level quality control, our dataset consisted of an RNA expression matrix for 535,248 SRT spots (observations) and RNA 28,965 genes (features).

Biological domains from the dlPFC were annotated with two parallel strategies. <a href="https://github.com/feiyoung/PRECAST" target="_blank">PRECAST</a> spatial clustering was used to define spatial domains (**domain~SP~**) that mirrored canonical dlPFC cytoarchitectural lamina. This strategy identified spatially contiguous regions with low UMI counts that clustered separately and were removed from downstream analyses. Domain~SP~ consisted of the following annotations: layers (L) L1, L2, L3/4, L5, L6, and white matter (WM). 

The second annotation strategy used existing snRNAseq data (<a href="https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adi5199" target="_blank">SZBDMulti-seq</a> dataset) and <a href="https://satijalab.org/seurat/reference/transferdata" target="_blank">Seurat label projection</a> to define cell type-driven domains (**domain~CT~**) that captured enriched signatures for cell types that often span across canonical dlPFC lamina. Domain~CT~ consisted of the following annotations: microglia and vascular cells (M/V), astrocytes (Ast), L2/3, L4, inhibitory neurons (Inb), L5, L6, and oligodendrocytes (Olg). It is important to note that these labels reflect spot-level transcriptional patterns and are not direct assessments of the abundance of the described cells within the SRT spots.
